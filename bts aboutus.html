💜 ABOUT BTS 💜

Who Are BTS?

BTS is a South Korean boy group that debuted in 2013 under Big Hit Entertainment. The group has seven members: RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook.

BTS is known for its music, powerful performances, meaningful lyrics, and messages about self-love, friendship, dreams, and personal growth.

Our Website

Welcome to BTS World! This website is dedicated to BTS and their amazing journey in the music industry. Here, fans can explore information about the members, view their photos, and learn more about the group.

Our goal is to celebrate BTS's music, achievements, and positive messages while sharing our appreciation for the seven talented members.

💜 About ARMY 💜

ARMY is the official name of BTS's fan community. Fans from different countries support BTS by enjoying their music, sharing their messages, and celebrating their achievements.

BTS and ARMY share a special connection built through music, encouragement, and mutual support.

Our Mission

Our mission is to introduce BTS to more people, celebrate their musical talents, and spread their inspiring messages of love, hope, and confidence.
